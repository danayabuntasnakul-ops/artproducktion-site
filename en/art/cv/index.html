01Statement

His work weaves the feeling of music into images, merging with landscape and the things around him — dissolving into ink lines that form a painting language entirely his own.

Danaya Buntasnakul is a Bangkok-based painter whose work moves between landscape, abstraction and illustration. For him, art is a way of releasing and rebalancing the mind: each painting mirrors an inner state rather than delivering a message.

Portrait photography is where he turns outward — a quiet, wordless conversation in which the sitter’s face carries feeling to the world. He has also sung as a founding tenor of the Suanplu Chorus and performed with the Anatta Theatre Troupe for more than a decade.

Three signature verbs

attention  ·  listening  ·  the slow search for meaning
