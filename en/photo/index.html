Approach

A portrait is a quiet, wordless conversation with the person in front of the lens.

Their face and the way they hold themselves carry feeling out into the world. So before the day of the shoot, we talk it through — the pictures you'd like, the place, the images you love. What comes out isn't a picture of just anyone. It's a picture of you, the way you really are.
