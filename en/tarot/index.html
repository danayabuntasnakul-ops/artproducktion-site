Approach

  • The cards are a mirror that lets you see yourself more clearly
  • Not predicting, but reflecting together
  • The final answer is always yours

A reading moves step by step, so that every question is given its own time.

  1. Tarot cards are laid out one question at a time, looking at the same matter from several sides.
  2. A single Oracle card closes the reading, to sum up the direction.
The Reader

About the reader

Every art form is bound up with being human. A painting records what the eye sees and the heart feels; a photograph holds the moment when a person is most themselves; music and words reach the things that can't be said outright.

Dan — Danaya Buntasnakul — works in painting, illustration, photography and writing, and has taught art at university. Each of these tools leads him back to the same question: how do they open up the way we see the world, and ourselves?

Reading tarot is one more way into that understanding. The images on the cards make room for what we keep to ourselves to be spoken. Each time the cards are turned, the person who brings the question and the reader both learn something, and come to understand a little more of the humanity that lives in themselves and in each other.

Begin with a single question, then look at it together, slowly.
